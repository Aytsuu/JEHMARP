## Objective

Implement one public **Privacy & Data Privacy Notice** at `/privacy` for
JEHMARP's Philippines-facing website. Do not create a duplicate
`/data-privacy` page: a privacy policy is the public notice, while data privacy
is the broader operational compliance program behind it.

The notice must be designed for the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, its
Implementing Rules and Regulations, and National Privacy Commission guidance.
It must be clear, accessible, and available before a visitor submits personal
data. The final legal text and the business's actual practices require review by
the designated privacy contact/DPO or qualified Philippine counsel.

## Required implementation

### Public notice and navigation

1. Add an SSR public route at `/privacy` using the existing public layout and
   page-shell conventions. Title it **Privacy & Data Privacy Notice**.
2. Replace the placeholder Privacy Policy footer link with `/privacy`. Do not
   add a second Data Privacy link or a public Terms/Cookie link in this task.
3. The public notice must cover, in clear plain language:
   - identity and contact details of the Personal Information Controller;
   - categories of data collected: customer/order details, contact inquiries,
     reseller applications, account/agent data, payment references, IP address,
     user agent, session identifiers, and security/anti-bot data;
   - purposes: order fulfilment/support, reseller assessment, inquiry response,
     account administration, security/session handling, anti-spam prevention,
     operational records, and legal obligations;
   - applicable processing grounds, without incorrectly relying on consent for
     every required order, account, or security operation;
   - authorized recipients/processors: Cloudflare Workers, Turnstile, and KV;
     Supabase; Upstash; Resend; and authorized JEHMARP personnel;
   - possible cross-border processing by service providers and JEHMARP's
     accountability for data under its control;
   - configured retention periods, data-subject rights, the privacy request
     channel, security safeguards at a non-sensitive level, breach-response
     contact, notice version/effective date, and update procedure.

### Configuration and publication gate

4. Extend `PlatformSettings` with a focused immutable privacy-notice section:
   controller legal name, Philippine business address, dedicated privacy/DPO
   email, notice version, effective date, and distinct retention text for
   inquiries, reseller applications, orders, accounts, security logs, and
   backups.
5. Add matching defaults, normalization, patch/merge behavior, and an admin
   settings surface. Every field above is required before publishing.
6. Fail closed: the `/privacy` route and footer link must not be public until
   all required privacy-notice values are valid. Do not show a completed-looking
   notice with placeholders. The production release checklist must require an
   authorized administrator to enter and verify the actual legal values first.
7. Do not expose TINs, payment account data, secrets, internal security rules,
   or vendor API credentials in the public notice.

### Collection-time notice

8. Add concise, purpose-specific notices with a `/privacy` link at the guest
   order, contact inquiry, reseller application, login, and registration flows.
   The notice must appear before submission.
9. A required acknowledgement may confirm that the notice was read. Do not use
   it as blanket consent. Marketing consent, if introduced later, must be a
   separate, optional, unchecked control and must not affect orders, accounts,
   inquiries, or reseller applications.

### Cookies and optional tracking

10. The first release covers only necessary services: session/security storage
    and Cloudflare Turnstile. Describe those under a "Cookies and Similar
    Technologies" section in `/privacy`.
11. Do not add Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising tags, a cookie banner,
    a consent-preference database, or optional-tracking scripts in this task.
    A future optional analytics/advertising release requires a separate cookie
    policy, opt-in controls before loading those services, withdrawal controls,
    and vendor-specific disclosures.
